# HR_Attrition_Analytics_Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
HR Attrition Analysis is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee turnover trends and identify key factors contributing to attrition within an organization.

The dashboard enables HR teams and management to make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention and workforce planning.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition can lead to:

- Increased hiring and training costs
- Loss of experienced talent
- Reduced productivity
- Workforce instability

This dashboard helps identify:

- Departments with high attrition
- Job roles most affected by attrition
- Impact of salary, age, and experience on employee turnover
- Gender-based attrition distribution

---

# 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following major metrics:

- Total Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Salary
- Total Employees
- Years at Company

# 📈 Dashboard Insights

- Attrition count by Male and Female employees

## 4️⃣ Age Analysis
